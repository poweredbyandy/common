Send a quotation or sale order
==============================

#. Open a quotation (draft or sent) or a confirmed sale order.
#. Click **Send**.
#. Tick **Email**, **WhatsApp**, or both.
#. Click **Send**.

Email uses the standard quotation or order mail template. WhatsApp uses
``sale_quotation`` or ``sale_order``, and the customer needs a phone number
and an approved template.

**Send PRO-FORMA Invoice** stays email only. The header buttons
**WhatsApp Cotización** and **WhatsApp Orden** still send WhatsApp alone.
