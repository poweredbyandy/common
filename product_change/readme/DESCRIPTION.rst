This module lets you change a product from Goods to Service (and the other
way around), switch inventory tracking between No Tracking and Track by
Quantity, and change the unit of measure.

A button on the product form opens a wizard where you choose which of
those options to apply. Sales orders, purchase orders and invoices are
kept. Incoming and outgoing stock is rebuilt from existing moves or
generated from confirmed orders and posted invoices when the new
configuration requires it.
