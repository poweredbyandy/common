#. Open a product form (or select products in the list).
#. Click **Change Type / UoM** (Inventory / Administrator).
#. Tick the changes you need:

   * **Change Product Type**: Goods ? Service.
   * **Change Inventory Tracking**: No Tracking ? Track by Quantity.
   * **Change Unit of Measure**: choose the new unit. If it belongs to
     the same category, you can convert quantities and prices so
     document totals stay the same.
#. Click **Apply**.

Sales, purchases and invoices remain. When the product becomes a good or
starts tracking quantity, the wizard generates or rebuilds incoming and
outgoing stock from confirmed orders and posted invoices.
