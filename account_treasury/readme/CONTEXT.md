Invoices only cover part of the money a company expects to receive or pay.
Rent, payroll, services, taxes, loan instalments or expected sales are
known in advance but are not invoices yet, so the accounting dashboard
cannot show when cash will be needed.

This module keeps those expectations in their own models, independent from
invoices, and compares them with the payments that really happened. The
treasury team sees on a calendar what is due each day, what was already
paid or received, and how the month or the year is going.
