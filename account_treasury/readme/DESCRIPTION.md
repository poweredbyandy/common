This module adds a treasury planner that works apart from invoices.

Fixed expenses and recurring income are defined once as recurring
movements and turned into dated forecasts. Variable income and expenses
are registered as single forecasts. Each forecast can then be linked to
one or several real movements (posted payments or bank statement lines),
so partial payments are tracked until the forecast is fully paid.

A treasury calendar built with OWL shows every forecast and every payment
made on its day, with a chart of real and pending inflows and outflows and
the projected net flow. Amounts can be shown in any active currency,
converted at the rate of the document date or at today's rate.
