1. Open **Treasury > Calendar**. The month view shows each forecast on its
   date: green for inflows, red for outflows, with an icon for its status
   (pending, partially paid, paid or overdue). Payments made are shown in
   italics on the date they happened.
2. Use the toolbar to move between months or years, to pick the currency
   of the report and to convert at the document date rate or at today's
   rate. The chart above the calendar shows real and pending inflows and
   outflows and the projected net flow of the period.
3. Hover a day and click **+** to register a variable income or expense on
   that date.
4. Click a forecast to open it. In the **Real Movements** tab add a line,
   choose whether the source is a payment or a bank statement line and
   select it. The applied amount is proposed from the amount still due and
   the amount still free on the real movement, and can be reduced to apply
   a partial payment.
5. The forecast becomes **Partially Paid** or **Paid** automatically.
   Pending forecasts that are not needed anymore can be cancelled.
6. **Treasury > Operations > Payments Made** lists every real movement
   linked to a forecast. **Forecasts** also offers pivot and graph views.

Changing the amount, partner or category of a recurring movement updates
its future pending forecasts. Changing its recurrence or dates regenerates
them.
