1. Click the money icon in the systray.
2. Choose **Open** and confirm the counted cash. If it differs from the
   accounting balance, the difference is posted to the journal profit or
   loss account.
3. Use **Cash In** or **Cash Out** during the day. Each move creates a
   posted statement line on the cash journal.
4. Click **Status** to see the accounting balance, session totals and other
   journal items such as customer payments that also hit the cash account.
5. At the end of the day choose **Close**, count the drawer and confirm.
   The session stores the counted amount, posts any closing difference and
   creates the daily bank statement used by the accounting dashboard.

The same actions are available from **Cash Box > Sessions**.
