#. Set the company logo under *Settings > Companies*.
#. Open a product and choose **Print Labels**.
#. Select **ZPL Labels** (or **ZPL Labels with price**) and the quantity.
#. Confirm. The report returns ZPL text with the generated logo, product
   name, internal reference, company name/phone and barcode.
#. Send that ZPL to the thermal printer (Device Bridge or another raw
   printer).
