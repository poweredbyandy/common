#. Post a customer invoice or vendor bill.
#. Register a payment greater than the residual and keep the difference open
   (or park it on a reconciliable advance account).
#. Open the invoice again. Under the residual, an **Excess to Refund** widget
   appears (same style as outstanding payments) with a **Return** button.
#. Confirm the payment register wizard. You can refund only part of the excess
   and repeat **Return** later for the remaining amount.
#. Posted excess refunds appear as **Refunded on** lines. Use the info icon to
   view or cancel a specific refund.
#. The invoice stays paid; only the open excess is reduced or cleared.
