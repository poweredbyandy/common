To show catalog pricelist prices with taxes included:

1. Go to *Sales > Configuration > Settings*.
2. Enable **Precios del catálogo con impuestos**.
3. Save.

This applies to pricelist prices shown in the product catalog (sale orders and
standalone catalog menu).

To show or hide the amount outside the pricelist currency (for example VES
when the pricelist is in USD):

1. Go to *Sales > Configuration > Pricelists*.
2. Open the pricelist.
3. Enable or disable **Mostrar monto fuera de la lista**.
4. Save.

The option is enabled by default. When disabled, the catalog only shows the
pricelist currency amount.
