Adds a security group for product image managers. Users can update the main
product photo and website/eCommerce media photos.

They see only the Product Configurator app (product list and form). Contacts,
Discuss and other apps are hidden. On the form they only see the product name,
internal reference and internal code in read-only mode.

They cannot create, archive or delete products. Assign this group only to
dedicated users (do not combine it with Sales, Purchase or Inventory roles).
