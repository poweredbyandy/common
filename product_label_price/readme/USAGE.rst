#. Open a product and choose **Print Labels**.
#. Select a PDF format (2 x 7, 4 x 7, 4 x 12 or Dymo) and confirm.
#. If the company setting is on, the label includes the price from the
   selected pricelist.
#. If the setting is off, the label prints name, reference, barcode and
   QR without the price.
