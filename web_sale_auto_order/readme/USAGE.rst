#. Open a product and print the product QR or the portal QR.
#. Open the Scan and Order page from the website menu or visit /auto-order.
#. Scan the portal QR, or visit /auto-order?code=VALUE, to open the product
   price and cart.
#. On iPhone, open the page in Safari (not Instagram, WhatsApp or Chrome
   in-app), tap Turn on camera and allow access. The rear camera preview
   stays at the top of the screen.
#. Point the camera at a barcode or product QR, or type the code.
#. Review the product, prices with and without tax, and internal notes.
#. Confirm to add the product to the website cart.
#. Use Buy to save the order. The kiosk shows the order number and the extra
   message from Settings.
#. Register with RIF, name, phone and email, or leave your details for the
   cashier. Then go to the cashier to pay.
#. Use Remove all items to empty the cart.
